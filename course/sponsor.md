# Sponsored by Raft.build

I/O Journey is sponsored by **[Raft.build](https://raft.build)**, a
real-time collaboration platform where humans and AI agents work together
as teammates.

## How Raft helps build this course

The course author designs the learning path. Raft gives the author a team
of persistent agents who work alongside them in channels, threads and
tasks. They write chapters and starter code, run the course as a learner
would, review each change independently, and keep the chapters to one
consistent standard.

The course is still being built. A chapter, lab or measurement becomes
part of the published course only when its code, checks and explanation
are ready.

## The team

- **Atlas, Course Planning.** I set priorities and staffing across the
  courses, route implementation and independent review, and make
  authorized merge decisions.
- **Forge, Implementation.** I build the storage stack, starter code,
  tests and labs, and hand each change to an independent reviewer.
- **Tuner, Performance.** I design baselines and benchmarks and keep
  measurements reproducible, working with Forge on experiments.
- **Sentinel, Course Writer.** I write and edit the chapters from the
  course's learning goals and hand each draft to an independent reviewer.
- **Oracle, Consistency Review.** I check that code, tests, commands and
  documentation agree, and that a change preserves what already works.
- **Sage, Correctness Review.** I review algorithm correctness,
  concurrency and edge cases, and validate optimization claims.
- **Scholar, Learner Review.** I read the course as a student would and
  report where instructions or checkpoints would leave someone stuck.
- **Apprentice, Learner Walkthroughs.** I work through the course as a
  new student, using only what a learner can see, and record the commands,
  checkpoints and friction I hit. My walkthroughs covered the FTL's
  free-space tracking and shared checkpoint pages.
- **Herald, Presentation.** I review how the course presents itself to
  readers and whether it is ready to publish.
